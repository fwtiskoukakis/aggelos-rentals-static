Clear rental terms

Included kilometres

120 km/day or 2,000 km/month. Extra kilometres cost €0.20/km.

Insurance & VAT

VAT and comprehensive insurance with a €1,500 excess are included. The excess is your liability under the insurance terms; it is not a cash deposit.

No cash deposit

We do not hold a cash security deposit. The €1,500 insurance excess still applies. Ask us to confirm payment arrangements before booking.

Delivery

Free delivery in Piraeus. Delivery in Athens costs €30. We agree the exact meeting point and time; collection and return arrangements are confirmed separately.

Request your rental quote

Send your dates, preferred model, daily or monthly rental and delivery location. We will confirm availability, the total price and the meeting arrangements before you book. Opening WhatsApp prepares an enquiry; it does not confirm a reservation.
