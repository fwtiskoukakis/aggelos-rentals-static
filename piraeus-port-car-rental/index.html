Arrange your port meeting

  1. Send your arrival date, ferry or cruise details and the time you need the car.
  2. We confirm the available car, final price, exact meeting point and time.

Piraeus has multiple ferry gates and cruise terminals. The meeting point depends on your arrival and is agreed individually. We do not promise a fixed gate, immediate delivery or round-the-clock handovers.

Before you travel

Free Piraeus delivery applies to the agreed handover. Collection and return arrangements are confirmed separately. If you plan to take the car on a ferry, ask us to confirm permission and insurance terms before booking; a port handover does not automatically authorise ferry transport.

Clear rental terms

Included kilometres

120 km/day or 2,000 km/month. Extra kilometres cost €0.20/km.

Insurance & VAT

VAT and comprehensive insurance with a €1,500 excess are included. The excess is your liability under the insurance terms; it is not a cash deposit.

No cash deposit

We do not hold a cash security deposit. The €1,500 insurance excess still applies. Ask us to confirm payment arrangements before booking.

Delivery

Free delivery in Piraeus. Delivery in Athens costs €30. We agree the exact meeting point and time; collection and return arrangements are confirmed separately.
